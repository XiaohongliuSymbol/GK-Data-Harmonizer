# GK-Data-Harmonizer
A robust ETL pipeline to build the ground-truth dataset for China's Gaokao system. It transforms fragmented, heterogeneous data from official portals, university sites, and even social media into a unified, analysis-ready source of truth.

# More words
It's as if overnight, the market became saturated with "intelligent" Gaokao advisory systems. In reality, most are castles in the sand, their predictions built on a fragile foundation of unreliable, inconsistent, and shallow data. The principle is simple: Garbage In, Garbage Out.
GK-Data-Harmonizer is engineered to solve this foundational problem. It's not just another scraper; it is a sophisticated data ingestion and harmonization engine designed to tackle the real-world challenge of wrangling data from a complex ecosystem of sources—both official and community-driven.

We're talking about a chaotic landscape of data, from the structured portals of provincial Education Examination Authorities and university websites to the unstructured, often anecdotal, streams on platforms like Xiaohongshu. Each source presents a minefield of varying structures, questionable accuracy, and a complete lack of a unified standard of credibility.

How this harmonized data is ultimately presented is a story for another day. Our mission here is to forge that unshakeable foundation. Because in the overwhelming fog of the internet, ordinary people deserve a beacon of clarity—a single source of truth to anchor their hopes for the future.
